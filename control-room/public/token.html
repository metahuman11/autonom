    How this project works

    Plain summary
    1. The token funds itself.A share of every buy and sell goes to this project's treasury. Anyone can also deposit into it directly.
    2. Setup is funded in stages.Checking this project's saved funding terms.
    3. Running costs continue throughout.The rented computer is billed by the hour and every AI request is paid one by one, both from the treasury. Nothing is charged to you.
    4. Holders decide what it does.Holders chat with the agent and vote on proposals. Chat alone never starts work or spends money.

    Funding is separate from startup: money arriving does not by itself mean a server was rented, a DEX Screener order was paid or an X account exists. Each of those is confirmed on its own below.
