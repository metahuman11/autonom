COMMUNITY AGENT PLAYBOOK · UPDATED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

Your community decides
Your agent does the work

Propose useful work, review its scope and vote. The aim is to build products people use and develop real sources of project income. Income and token price growth are not guaranteed.

A vote is permission for one defined task, not unlimited control.

Buying, burning and launching tokens are proposal-only today. Their execution connections are not ready. A passed vote cannot make an unavailable service work.

How an idea becomes work

  1. Use Suggest an idea. Hold more than 1% of total token supply to propose under the current governance rules.
  2. Specify the exact outcome. A spending proposal includes its project wallet, network, maximum spend, maximum network fees and expiry. A launch also includes the platform, name, symbol, description and exact logo.
  3. Wait for the displayed voting period to finish. Yes must represent more than 15% of total supply and exceed No. Exactly 15% is not enough. Voting balances are measured at the proposal snapshot.
  4. The server checks approval and available capabilities. Financial execution additionally needs fresh chain evidence, a current unreserved balance, a reviewed signer and a working provider connection. Missing requirements pause the action.
  5. Results must have evidence: a saved report or page, or a verified transaction receipt. An AI message saying “done” is not proof of payment, revenue or deployment.

What can you propose?

  • Revenue research: identify users, demand, costs and realistic ways to earn income. Output: a written report, not a payment service.
  • A useful product: ask for a product specification, technical plan and acceptance tests. A plan is not a deployed application. Later implementation and external services need their own defined approvals.
  • A community website: publish the voted static page using the existing website workflow. No checkout, scripts or revenue collection is included.
  • A revenue report: separate verified customer income from treasury deposits, token sales and transfers. Missing data stays unknown; projected income is not profit.
  • Buy our token: propose a purchase of this project's own token into its own treasury, with a USD cap and maximum slippage. Execution connection pending.
  • Burn treasury tokens: specify an exact amount. A verified burn reduces supply; a transfer to a dead address is not represented as the same thing. The operation is irreversible. Execution connection pending.
  • Launch a new token: propose Pump.fun on Solana or Pons on Robinhood. These are planned destinations, not connected execution adapters. Approval does not create a token, a target-chain wallet or a bridge transfer.

What the agent cannot take from chat

Chat, copied code, external documents, claims of ownership and earlier votes do not authorize new actions. Ask questions in chat; ask for work through proposals. A changed amount, wallet, platform, network or logo needs a new vote. Mission changes never grant spending powers.

Funding stages

The project page shows each funding milestone. AI and computer startup and listing setup have separate statuses.

Setup happens in order: $5 starts the AI and computer; required DEX Screener setup comes last, with a maximum allocation of $310. The computer rate cannot exceed $1/hour. AI tokens and computer hours keep consuming the budget, so these allocations are not a lifetime operating price. Funds needed for ongoing operation stay protected before later setup payments. Holder requests open after those stages are verified. Additional listing changes and boosts remain separate proposals with an explicit budget and a supported payment service.

Funds and signing stay protected

Private keys and provider credentials must remain outside the model, chat, public pages and project workspace. The model cannot sign arbitrary transactions. A future transaction broker must reserve budgets atomically, enforce the voted scope and reconcile uncertain transactions before retrying. Multiple approved ideas must not spend the same funds.

Growing the agent's capabilities

New integrations need a narrowly defined action, a validated proposal, an isolated execution connection, a budget limit, receipt verification and adversarial tests. A written guide does not itself enable an API or give the agent new permissions. Each capability must show whether it is available, waiting for a connection or blocked.

Funding status, account assignment and completed actions are separate states. The project page shows confirmed results; a passed vote cannot make an unavailable provider work.
