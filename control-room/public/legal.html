Autonom · Terms of use and privacy notice · version 2026-09-30

Terms of use

Autonom is a website where a token community funds and directs an AI agent. Using the site means you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, do not use the site.

Operator details

The legal operator's name, registered address and contact channel will be listed here. Until then the site is operated as a pre-launch service and support requests are answered through the project pages.

1. What the service does

  • You can launch a token from your own wallet on a third-party venue (pump.fun on Solana, Pons on Robinhood Chain). The venue's own contract holds the token and its bonding curve; Autonom never holds your token or your first purchase.
  • Each project has an AI wallet (its treasury). Autonom keeps that wallet's key in its own custody. Project funds pay for machine rental, AI usage and the project's required startup services, or actions authorized through the platform's community controls. Treasury actions are restricted by the project's spending rules.
  • The AI agent ("Kurt") answers questions, writes reports and publishes inert static pages. It holds no keys, cannot send transactions and cannot start work from chat. Required startup services follow the project's setup policy. Custom holder tasks require an approved proposal. Chat alone cannot authorize a payment.

2. Fees and costs

  • Autonom takes no platform fee on launches or trades. The venue's own fees apply, including its launch and trading fees and any creator fee or creator tax. Review the terms and quote shown before signing.
  • The creator fee or creator tax of a project flows to that project's AI wallet. Under the current staged startup policy, $5 funds initial AI and computer usage, followed by required DEX Screener setup with a maximum allocation of $310. The computer rate is capped at $1/hour. Computer hours and AI usage remain metered expenses; the setup allocations are not a lifetime operating price. Existing obligations are handled under the project's applicable policy. When usable funds are insufficient, work can pause; restarting depends on funding, project status and provider availability.
  • Network fees and the venue's launch fee are paid by your wallet when you sign.

3. Risks and no advice

  • Tokens can lose all their value. Nothing on this site is investment, legal or tax advice, and nothing is a promise of income or price.
  • Third-party venues, chains, wallets and hosting providers can fail or change their rules; Autonom does not control them.
  • The AI agent can make mistakes. Its outputs are records of work, not guarantees.

4. Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for your wallet, keys and passwords. Never share them with anyone, including in chat.
  • Do not use the site for unlawful purposes, to launch tokens that infringe others' rights, or to attack the service.
  • Chat and proposals are public. Do not post content you do not have the right to publish.

5. Changes and availability

The service is provided as is. Features can change, pause or end. These terms can be updated; the version date above changes when they do.

Privacy notice

What is stored

  • Wallet addresses you connect or that appear on chain, and the messages, proposals, votes and comments you sign. These are public on the project pages.
  • Chat sessions: a short-lived session cookie after you approve chat with your wallet. It expires within an hour.
  • Server logs: IP address, time and requested page, kept for security and troubleshooting and deleted on rotation.
  • Project content the agent produces (reports, pages) and the project's funding ledger.

What is not stored

  • No private keys, seed phrases or passwords of yours. No e-mail unless you send one to the support contact.
  • No advertising trackers and no third-party analytics scripts on the site.

Third parties

Launch transactions go to the venue you chose and to the blockchain; AI requests go to the AI provider; machines are rented from hosting providers. Each of them handles data under its own terms.

Your choices

Disconnecting your wallet ends the chat session. Signed on-chain records cannot be deleted. For anything else, use the support contact once it is listed above.
